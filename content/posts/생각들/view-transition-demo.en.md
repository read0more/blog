## From the list to the article

Open this post from the home page, the Thoughts category, or the Rendering tag. The card title briefly moves into the position and size of the article title. Check desktop and 390px mobile layouts to see that a multiline title does not overlap the article body.

## Back to the list

Use the browser's Back button to return to the previous list. This keeps the browser and router's normal history and scroll restoration, which may restore the page without a title animation.

The category and tag links above the article and the read0more link in the header also lead back to lists, connecting the same title in the reverse direction. Titles outside the viewport and pages that have not loaded can navigate without an animation. The header, sidebar, and article body do not slide across the screen.

## Motion preferences and language

Enable reduced motion in the operating system or browser to navigate without transition animations. Links and article content continue to work in browsers without the View Transitions API.
